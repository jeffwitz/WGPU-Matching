          Direct-pair tests on supplied large-deformation images
                   Working addendum to the DIC initialization manuscript

                                         14 November 2026


1     Question and evidence boundary
The two supplied image sets test initialization of digital image correlation (DIC) without temporal
information. Every target is paired directly with its own Img_F reference. Spatial propagation within
a pair is permitted; solutions from another target are never reused. Reference-image preprocessing
may be cached.
    The first set, labelled FEA, contains 191 deformed images of a perforated plate, each 1024 × 1024
pixels. The second, labelled ClosedFormExpression, contains 500 targets of a strip, each 1024×5632
pixels. The reference strip occupies only the first 512 rows. These labels are retained as supplied,
without claiming verified generating formulas.
    The displacement archive contains 51 triangular-mesh states and a script describing a cross-
shaped geometry. It provides no documented correspondence to the 191 plate images or the 500 strip
images, nor a physical-to-pixel coordinate map. Therefore no displacement-error or mechanically
correct maximum deformation can yet be reported. The present measurements concern numerical
acceptance and final photometric alignment. A high correlation is not independent displacement
truth.


2     Fixed protocol and separate comparator
The manuscript pipeline uses learned coarse correspondences, image-derived affine proposals, local
zero-mean normalized cross-correlation (ZNCC), and inverse-compositional Gauss–Newton (IC-GN)
solves with neighbour confirmation and propagation. Its nonlinear learned residual remains disabled.
Model rasters are 192 × 192 for the plate and 128 × 704 for the strip, preserving aspect ratio and
bounding the global feature grid to 5632 tokens. The final solver uses native pixels, 64-pixel supports
and stride, bilinear interpolation, 30 iterations, tolerance 10−3 and minimum ZNCC 0.65. The same
weights and consistency settings are retained.
    The image-only contour comparator is specified in detail in Section 3. Its purpose is to supply
starting scale and translation, with no claim of contour-derived interior displacement truth.


3     Silhouette-extent initialization: formulation and executed algo-
      rithm
3.1   Purpose and information available to the estimator
The comparator supplies a large-displacement starting transformation from the observable extent of
the specimen. It does not match contour landmarks, describe the speckle, or estimate a nonlinear
interior displacement from the boundary. Its four unknowns are two axis-aligned scale factors and
two translations. This deliberately restricted hypothesis tests whether the local solver can recover
useful states once the order of magnitude of the motion is provided by the images.
    Only the reference image I0 and the current target I1 enter this initialization. Neither the target
index, a prescribed strain, the displacement files, nor a preceding target solution is used. The
complete reference preparation may be cached, but the target states are reset for every pair. The

final field is a set of independently resolved local affine states, not the global diagonal map imposed
as a final measurement.

3.2   Image-derived material masks
Pixel centres have integer coordinates (x, y), with x increasing to the right and y downwards. In each
image separately, the background value is read from the bottom-right pixel, bj = Ij (Wj − 1, Hj − 1).
Let Ej = {(x, y) : Ij (x, y) = bj }. We label its four-connected components Cjk and define
                                                          [
                                       Mj = Ωj \                     Cjk .                          (1)
                                                     k: |Cjk |≥128

Thus large constant-background regions, including sufficiently large internal holes, are excluded.
Small islands with the same quantized intensity are kept: otherwise individual speckle pixels would
perforate the mask and remove many usable supports. The threshold is a component area in native
pixels, not an intensity tolerance. There is no morphological closing, learned segmentation, subpixel
contour fit or analytical material mask in this operation.
    The reference mask M0 defines the region of interest (ROI), meaning the domain eligible for
correlation. A window is eligible only when its full reference support is contained in that mask. The
target mask M1 supplies its bounding box; it is not passed as a target-material validity constraint to
the solver. Target-image bounds are checked by the solver, but material occlusion is not independently
certified. Exact equality to a single background intensity is suitable for these rendered images, not a
general segmentation rule for noisy experimental backgrounds.

3.5    Status-based selection and confirmation of seeds
Two hypotheses are tested in the fixed order: zero displacement/gradient, then the silhouette proposal.
On the regular eligible-window grid, every second row and every second column are sampled. The
identical sparse subset is solved under each hypothesis. Finite solver-accepted states are counted;
the hypothesis with the larger count wins. A tie selects the first, zero hypothesis. No displacement
truth, correlation-error ground truth or target index enters this ranking. The accepted counts in
this selection are not yet the final propagated counts.
    Let di = (ui , vi )⊤ and let j be an eight-neighbour of i. The first-order prediction transported
from i to j is
                                  dbj|i = di + Gi (xj − xi ), G
                                                              b = Gi .
                                                                j|i                                (8)
Neighbour agreement uses the Euclidean displacement norm and the Frobenius gradient norm:
                             ∥dj − dbj|i ∥2 ≤ 8 mpx,       ∥Gj − Gi ∥F ≤ 0.25.                                (9)
The implementation has two confirmation routes. A candidate anchor and an already verified
adjacent state are confirmed if they satisfy these tests. Otherwise eligible, not-yet-verified neighbours
are solved from transported states; at least two successful and consistent neighbour solves confirm
the parent and those neighbours. Solver status and finite parameters are also required. Batched
confirmation is enabled and used when more than eight candidate anchors exist; results are replayed
in the original confirmation order.
    The argument minimum_anchors=3 only produces a diagnostic indicating whether each eligible
grid component contains three confirmed anchors. In this comparator it does not reject a pair or
disable propagation. It must not be described as an enforced three-anchor acceptance gate.

3.6    Frontier propagation and its precise stopping rule
Each propagation pass starts from an immutable snapshot of accepted windows. For an unresolved
eligible window, the first two available accepted neighbours are considered in the fixed order up,
left, right, down, then the four diagonals. The transported proposal is solved on the images. A
finite, solver-accepted result is retained only if it also satisfies Equation (9) relative to that parent
prediction. The second parent is attempted only when the first has not produced an accepted result.
New states become parents on the next pass; accepted states remain unchanged. Propagation stops
if no frontier exists or a complete pass adds no accepted window.
    This is the existing confirmed-propagation policy: it is not a median over all neighbours, not
inverse-distance averaging, and not the historical three-attempt frontier policy. There is no fixed
three-attempt lifetime cap here. Neighbourhoods use the eligible-window grid, not an explicit
pixel-level material-connectivity test along every link. The reference support mask prevents solving
windows over holes but does not prove that every diagonal link avoids a narrow fissure. Discontinuity
preservation is therefore not established by this experiment.

3.7   Frozen settings and observed stages
The solver operates on native grayscale pixels with affine motion, bilinear interpolation, at most
30 iterations, tolerance 10−3 , and minimum zero-mean normalized cross-correlation (ZNCC) 0.65.
Its separate outlier policy is disabled; the above neighbour consistency checks remain active. The
64-pixel experiment uses support and stride 64; the separate 32-pixel experiment uses both 32. No
angular bank, learned residual, separate local correlation search, regional retry, or coarse-to-fine
support transfer is used by this comparator.

             Pair and support       Zero trial   Box trial   Confirmed seeds   Final accepted
             Plate 191, 32 pixels           0            4                7            18/36
             Strip 500, 64 pixels           0            8               35          109/128

Both examples require six propagation passes, including the stopping pass when applicable. Confirmed
seeds can outnumber sparse trial acceptances because neighbour confirmation performs additional
solves. These counts come directly from the saved per-pair records. They show that the final
coverage is not simply the fraction accepted under an uncorrected box map. They do not establish
the correctness of the accepted displacement fields.

3.8   Interpretation, failure modes and computational scope
The useful role of the silhouette is to turn a difficult global texture search into a plausible starting
scale and translation. If several windows fall inside the solver’s convergence domain, their solved
gradients can transport initializations locally, without forcing the entire specimen to follow the
diagonal box map. Conversely, a correct envelope does not guarantee any correct interior seed.
Different non-affine fields can share the same bounding box, including fields with severe interior
localization.
    Cropping, occlusion, detached components, rotation, shear, an incorrectly identified corner
background, or a few remote segmentation outliers can bias the extrema. The method has no
built-in uncertainty estimate for these effects. For one coordinate s = L1 /L0 , first-order perturbation
gives δs ≃ δL1 /L0 − L1 δL0 /L20 ; δt = δℓ1 − sδℓ0 − ℓ0 δs. Short reference extents and large scale
ratios amplify boundary uncertainty. These limitations preclude presenting the method as a general
replacement for feature matching or as a deformation measurement from contours.
    Mask labelling and box extraction are host-side array operations. Hypothesis selection, confirma-
tion and propagation are orchestrated in Python; the local solves use the WebGPU graphics interface
and WebGPU Shading Language (WGSL) kernels. Parameter/status arrays cross the host–device
boundary between calls. This experiment is therefore not a wholly graphics-resident pipeline, and it
does not establish real-time performance. Its reproducible role is a non-learned, image-only control
within the same local solver.
    The new evidence motivates adding this proposal as an optional candidate when complete
specimen silhouettes are available. It does not establish novelty or general superiority. Indepen-
dent displacement truth, segmentation perturbation tests, and paired ablations of proposal-only,
confirmation and propagation are still required before attributing a quantified accuracy gain to the
silhouette itself.

5    A diagnosed mismatch between the pipeline and these images
In the plate reference, 2125 of 2304 coarse queries lie on the uniform background; for the strip the
count is 5120 of 5632. The global affine validator requires inliers in at least four cells of a 3 × 3
grid covering the entire image. Material queries occupy at most two and three cells, respectively. A
material-only affine fit cannot meet this full-raster coverage guard. The existing pipeline is evaluated
unchanged: these failures must not be interpreted solely as a descriptor’s intrinsic inability to
recognize deformed speckle.
    The contour control separates discovery of a useful global starting scale from the subsequent
photometric solution. It is deliberately simple and specific to these images. It is neither an analytical
oracle nor a reproduction of a published feature-based method.


6    Fields, remapping and difficult supports
Only solver-accepted windows are plotted and remapped. Missing regions are shown explicitly rather
than completed by interpolation. The detailed patch illustrations select the accepted window with the
lowest recomputed final correlation, not the most attractive example. Absolute intensity differences
are not displacement-error maps. The original TIFF images and saved final states determine every
panel.


7    Reproducibility and consequences for the manuscript
The result directory contains per-pair commands, final six-parameter states, masks, seed provenance,
source and image fingerprints, all 51 inspected displacement files’ metadata, and scripts regenerating
the tables, figures and animations. A triangle-field evaluator has been tested at exact pixel centres
with explicit anisotropic coordinate maps and axis reversal; it is not applied to these images without
an established association.
    The learned and contour timings have different process-startup and reference-cache scopes and
are not used to claim an acceleration factor. No training or production thresholds were changed.

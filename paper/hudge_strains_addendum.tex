\section{Question and evidence boundary}
The two supplied image sets test initialization of digital image correlation (DIC) without temporal information. Every target is paired directly with its own \texttt{Img\_F} reference. Spatial propagation within a pair is permitted; solutions from another target are never reused. Reference-image preprocessing may be cached.

The first set, labelled \texttt{FEA}, contains 191 deformed images of a perforated plate, each $1024\times1024$ pixels. The second, labelled \texttt{ClosedFormExpression}, contains 500 targets of a strip, each $1024\times5632$ pixels. The reference strip occupies only the first 512 rows. These labels are retained as supplied, without claiming verified generating formulas.

Only the perforated plate and the strip are tested here; no cross-shaped specimen is part of these experiments. The separate \texttt{DEPL} archive is excluded: it was not used to generate initialization, run the solver, or score displacement errors for either image series. No independently associated displacement truth has been established for these images. The present measurements concern numerical acceptance and final photometric alignment, not a certified maximum recoverable strain. A high correlation is not independent displacement truth.

\section{Fixed protocol and separate comparator}
The manuscript pipeline uses learned coarse correspondences, image-derived affine proposals, local zero-mean normalized cross-correlation (ZNCC), and inverse-compositional Gauss--Newton (IC-GN) solves with neighbour confirmation and propagation. Its nonlinear learned residual remains disabled. Model rasters are $192\times192$ for the plate and $128\times704$ for the strip, preserving aspect ratio and bounding the global feature grid to 5632 tokens. The final solver uses native pixels, 64-pixel supports and stride, bilinear interpolation, 30 iterations, tolerance $10^{-3}$ and minimum ZNCC 0.65. The same weights and consistency settings are retained.

The image-only contour comparator is specified in detail in Section~\ref{sec:silhouette}. Its purpose is to supply starting scale and translation, with no claim of contour-derived interior displacement truth.

\section{Operational limits, not accuracy limits}
Table~\ref{tab:limits} gives the largest tested image indices reaching each acceptance fraction. The contour comparator evaluates every target at both supports. The learned pipeline uses a coarse level sweep followed by direct-pair tests near its loss of seeds. Coverage is not monotone, so a maximum does not guarantee success at every lower index. The targets reuse one texture per set and cannot be treated as independent statistical samples.

At the last supplied states, observed vertical extent ratios are $1015/211\simeq4.81$ for the plate and $5632/512=11$ for the strip. These envelope ratios are not local material stretches or prescribed strain levels.

A known zero-motion control on the strip accepts 98 of 128 mask-admissible 64-pixel windows. Its accepted parameters have maximum absolute value below $6\times10^{-8}$. The remaining 30 centres lie on the top row and lateral edge columns; the solver enforces a two-pixel margin on transformed target supports. The mask denominator is retained. This identity result is not a bound for nonzero motion, which can move supports away from the edges.

\section{A diagnosed mismatch between the pipeline and these images}
In the plate reference, 2125 of 2304 coarse queries lie on the uniform background; for the strip the count is 5120 of 5632. The global affine validator requires inliers in at least four cells of a $3\times3$ grid covering the entire image. Material queries occupy at most two and three cells, respectively. A material-only affine fit cannot meet this full-raster coverage guard. The existing pipeline is evaluated unchanged: these failures must not be interpreted solely as a descriptor's intrinsic inability to recognize deformed speckle.

The contour control separates discovery of a useful global starting scale from the subsequent photometric solution. It is deliberately simple and specific to these images. It is neither an analytical oracle nor a reproduction of a published feature-based method.

\section{Reproducibility and consequences for the manuscript}
Per-pair commands, final six-parameter states, masks, seed provenance, image and source fingerprints, and all displacement-file metadata are archived with the figure generators. The triangle-field evaluator is tested with explicit anisotropic coordinate maps and axis reversal, but no undocumented image--mesh association is assumed. Timings have different startup and reference-cache scopes and do not establish an acceleration factor. No training, production threshold or historical measurement changed. These external cases expose an initialization limitation and a useful image-only control; displacement accuracy, experimental metrology and comparisons with published methods remain open. The main manuscript's accuracy conclusions are unchanged.

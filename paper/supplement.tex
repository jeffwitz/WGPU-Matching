Inspection in the project's experimental environment confirms the 32-component encoder and contextual layers. The source snapshot, training manifest and present checkpoint hash are recorded in \path{audit/source_manifest.json}. The weights remain external to the paper sources. This identifies the present file without inventing an experiment-time hash.

Current native solver-source inspection finds an incremental corner-change convergence test followed by the correlation threshold; reference preparation also checks support and Hessian validity. Target or reference norm below $10^{-9}$ rejects a solve, and an incremental affine determinant magnitude below $10^{-8}$ rejects inversion. These are observations of the inspected external source, not proof that every historical binary used precisely those branches. The archived wrapper supplies 30 iterations, $10^{-3}$ tolerance, minimum ZNCC 0.65, affine motion and linear interpolation. It reads status greater than 0.5. The confirmatory policy additionally checks finite parameters and the 8-pixel/0.25 consistency limits. No claim of full independent post-update residual reevaluation is inferred from a convergence status.

The shader computes local ZNCC from sums of intensity, squared intensity and products, with variance floor $10^{-12}$ and invalid candidate sentinel $-2$. Returned scores and margins do not become a threshold in the qualifier. Bilinear local-field derivatives and native conversions are independently checked by the small documentation validation script, without training or changing the algorithm.

\section{Interpreting the evidence package}
The per-window recount independently recomputes the four causal totals from saved states and truth and checks the saved correctness masks. It reproduces the single initial false acceptance at index 1587 of pair 16051009/16091009. It verifies confirmation and support-study totals from their saved arrays. Re-reading archives is a consistency audit, not new statistical replication. Historical intermediate score volumes are not stored everywhere; the complete-stage figure uses archived initial states rather than fabricating missing correlation peaks.

Sources inspected today establish current implementations. Historical protocol dictionaries establish requested settings. Without experiment-time source snapshots and external solver revisions, their exact identity cannot be proved. The audit deliberately retains this distinction. Hardware latency and mask-interface guarantees must not be extrapolated from the numerical parity records.
